# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Our expectations

- Be kind, respectful, and constructive in discussions.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Focus on technical issues and collaborative problem solving.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers through the repository issue tracker or another appropriate channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate to protect the project community, including warnings, temporary removal, or permanent bans.
